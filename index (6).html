01 / 03 · NVIDIA compute

The work happens on your hardware, on a network outside Ratinole.

You run a supported NVIDIA GPU through an external compute network, with that network's own software. Its customers send jobs; your card does the work.

Ratinole runs no jobs and pays no rewards. Connecting a wallet here never starts, stops, or changes anything on your node.

NoteJob traffic is the compute network's, not Ratinole's. This page does not show a live job count.

02 / 03 · Rewards and costs

Rewards come in. Costs come out. Every assumption stays visible.

The network reports token rewards for finished jobs. Ratinole sets them against your estimated electricity cost and the reserve you plan to keep for operating the machine.

03 / 03 · NVDA accumulation

What remains can go toward NVDA, one approved swap at a time.

NVDA is Robinhood's stock token for NVIDIA, an ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain issued under Robinhood's terms. It is not a registered NVIDIA share, and GPU jobs do not pay in it.

You choose the amount. Ratinole fetches a quote, builds and simulates the transaction, and your wallet asks for your approval. Nothing is swapped on its own, and your reserve never leaves your wallet.

How a swap runs

Priced and checked before you sign.

Uniswap v3 · Robinhood ChainNo Ratinole fee
  1. You enter an amount of ETH or USDG. The quoter contract prices every direct and two-step route into NVDA.
  2. The contracts are checked on chain: the NVDA token, USDG, the pool and the router. Swaps stay off if any check fails.
  3. For USDG, your wallet first approves exactly the amount you chose, nothing more.
  4. The swap is simulated against the chain. Only then does your wallet ask you to sign.
  5. It counts as done once the chain confirms it. You get a transaction link.

The share and the token, side by side.

NVDA is Robinhood's stock token with NVIDIA-linked exposure. Ratinole shows what it trades at on Robinhood Chain next to the NVIDIA share reference, each with its source and its time.

What it is

A stock token issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited as an ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain. Its price follows NVIDIA, and dividends and splits are handled by a multiplier on the token.

What it is not

Not a directly registered NVIDIA share. It carries no shareholder voting rights, and holding it is governed by Robinhood's terms, not by NVIDIA.

Who can hold it

Robinhood says it may not be offered to U.S. persons, and sales are also restricted in places such as Canada, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Ratinole cannot check who you are; you confirm it before each swap.

Workflow and transparency

What is read, what is estimated, and what needs you.

Ratinole is an analytics and allocation interface. It is not a GPU network, a broker, a stock issuer, or a yield product.

01

What Ratinole reads

  • Public job records, reported rewards and uptime for a node address you enter
  • The NOS price, and a node's public record when the API answers
  • Your wallet's ETH, USDG and NVDA balances, after you connect
  • The NVDA contract, the issuer's record of it, and current prices
02

What it estimates

  • Electricity cost, from the watts, hours and price you enter
  • A net operating result, with the assumptions listed beside it
  • How much NVDA an amount would buy, from a quote that expires in seconds
  • Progress toward a goal, on inputs you can edit
03

What needs your approval

  • Connecting a wallet, which shares your public address only
  • Every swap, one transaction at a time, in your own wallet
  • Never a seed phrase, a private key, or standing permission to spend
04

What others provide

  • Compute jobs and rewards: the external compute network (Nosana)
  • Quotes and swaps: Uniswap v3 contracts on Robinhood Chain
  • NVDA itself, and its terms: its issuer, Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited
  • Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663), through an RPC endpoint
